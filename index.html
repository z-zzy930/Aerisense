Why everyday mealtime trends get missed

Swallowing stability shifts with fatigue, illness, posture, alertness, and texture—while formal checkups happen far apart.

The Gap Between Checkups

Brief clinical assessments and occasional speech therapist visits give snapshots, while scattered handwritten notes make multi-meal changes hard to compare systematically.

The AeriSense Approach

Calibrates to each resident's Personal Mealtime Signature, filters motion and contact artifacts, pairs caregiver context, and highlights recurring deviations for review.
