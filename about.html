Mission & Team

A Breath of Relief

We build personalized longitudinal mealtime monitoring to help care teams and clinicians spot recurring swallowing changes between scheduled evaluations.

Why We Built AeriSense

In Hong Kong aged-care and rehabilitation settings, swallowing stability can vary across meals and days while formal checks happen infrequently. By comparing ordinary meals against each person's calibrated baseline and pairing caregiver context, AeriSense supports clearer, calmer care decisions.
